About Green Growth - GROOT

A smart plant monitoring system built with Flask and Firebase Firestore for optimal plant growth.

Features
  • Plant Monitoring: Real-time temperature, humidity, and light tracking
  • Smart Irrigation: Automated pump control for optimal watering
  • Data Analytics: Historical sensor data and growth insights
  • Mobile Responsive: Monitor your plants from anywhere
  • Smart Alerts: Notifications for optimal growing conditions
How this helps you
  • Simple Monitoring: See your plants' conditions at a glance
  • Automated Care: The system can automate watering based on thresholds
  • Insights: View historical trends to improve plant health
  • Responsive UI: Works on desktop and mobile devices
Getting Started

To get started, contact your administrator to receive access credentials and deployment instructions specific to your environment. This application is maintained by the Green Growth team and configured centrally for production deployments.
